# Target Users

The Trip Tracker system will be used by several different groups within the company. Each group has different responsibilities and needs from the system.

Understanding these users is essential to designing workflows that match real operational processes.

---

## 1. Dispatchers

Dispatchers are the primary operational users of the system.

They are responsible for coordinating daily transport operations and ensuring that customer orders are executed successfully.

### Responsibilities

- Receive transport orders from customers
    
- Plan trips and assign drivers and vehicles
    
- Monitor the progress of active trips
    
- Communicate with drivers during execution
    
- Handle delays, changes, or operational issues
    
- Ensure that deliveries are completed successfully
    

### Needs

Dispatchers require:

- A clear overview of all trips (planned, active, completed)
    
- The ability to quickly assign drivers and vehicles
    
- Visibility into the current status of each trip
    
- Access to trip history and events
    
- Quick filtering and search for operational data
    

The system should reduce the need for manual coordination and improve situational awareness.

---

## 2. Drivers

Drivers execute the physical transport of goods.

They interact with the system primarily to view their assigned trips and report operational status updates.

### Responsibilities

- Execute assigned transport trips
    
- Arrive at pickup and delivery locations
    
- Load and unload goods
    
- Report relevant events during the trip
    
- Provide delivery confirmation (Proof of Delivery)
    

### Needs

Drivers require a simple and fast interface that allows them to:

- View assigned trips
    
- See pickup and delivery instructions
    
- Report trip status changes
    
- Upload proof of delivery
    

Because drivers operate under time pressure and often on mobile devices, the interface must remain simple and efficient.

---

## 3. Accounting Staff

Accounting personnel are responsible for preparing invoices and handling financial documentation.

Although they do not participate in operational planning, they rely on accurate trip data and delivery documentation.

### Responsibilities

- Prepare invoices based on completed transport orders
    
- Verify that deliveries have been completed
    
- Ensure Proof of Delivery is available
    
- Resolve billing disputes with customers
    

### Needs

Accounting staff require access to:

- Completed trips and their associated orders
    
- Delivery timestamps
    
- Proof of Delivery documents
    
- Exportable operational data
    

The system should ensure that delivery documentation is consistently stored and easily accessible.

---

## 4. System Administrators

Administrators are responsible for managing system access and configuration.

### Responsibilities

- Manage user accounts
    
- Assign roles and permissions
    
- Maintain basic system configuration
    
- Monitor system usage
    

### Needs

Administrators require:

- Tools to manage user accounts
    
- Role-based permission management
    
- Visibility into system configuration

# Problem Statement

## 1. Operational Context

NordCargo Logistics GmbH is a mid-sized road freight transportation company operating approximately 60 trucks and employing around 85 drivers. The company performs national and cross-border shipments for various industrial and commercial customers.

Daily operations are coordinated by dispatchers who manage customer orders, assign drivers and vehicles, and monitor the progress of trips until delivery is completed.

Currently, these operations are supported primarily by:

- Excel spreadsheets
    
- Phone calls
    
- Messaging apps (e.g., WhatsApp)
    
- Paper-based delivery notes and documents
    
- Informal tracking methods
    

While these tools work at a basic level, they create significant operational inefficiencies and risks as the company grows.

---

## 2. Core Problems

### Lack of Real-Time Operational Visibility

Dispatchers do not have a centralized system showing:

- Which trips are currently active
    
- Which driver is assigned to which trip
    
- The current status of a trip (loading, driving, delivery, etc.)
    
- Whether a delivery has been completed
    

This often leads to situations where dispatchers must call drivers directly to determine the status of a shipment.

---

### Fragmented and Inconsistent Information

Trip-related information is spread across multiple tools and channels:

- Order details in Excel
    
- Driver updates via phone or chat
    
- Delivery confirmations as paper documents
    
- Customer references in emails
    

This fragmentation leads to:

- Lost or inconsistent information
    
- Duplicate data entry
    
- Increased risk of errors
    

---

### Missing or Delayed Proof of Delivery (POD)

Proof of Delivery is critical for billing and resolving customer disputes.

Currently:

- POD documents are often received late
    
- POD images may be sent via messaging apps
    
- Documents may be lost or difficult to associate with a specific order
    

This causes delays in billing and increases administrative overhead.

---

### Inefficient Dispatcher Workflows

Dispatchers spend significant time on tasks such as:

- Searching for order information
    
- Calling drivers to check trip status
    
- Manually updating spreadsheets
    
- Tracking down missing documents
    

This reduces their ability to focus on higher-value activities such as planning and customer service.

---

### Limited Traceability and Auditability

Because trip status updates are informal, it is difficult to determine:

- When a truck arrived at a location
    
- When loading started or finished
    
- When a delivery was completed
    
- Who reported which information
    

This creates challenges in resolving disputes and analyzing operational performance.

---

## 3. Business Impact

The problems described above lead to:

- Reduced operational efficiency
    
- Lower service reliability
    
- Delayed invoicing due to missing documentation
    
- Increased administrative workload
    
- Difficulty scaling operations as the company grows
    

---

## 4. Desired Outcome

NordCargo requires a centralized **Trip Tracking System** that provides a reliable, structured overview of transport operations.

The system should allow dispatchers, drivers, and accounting staff to work from the same source of truth and ensure that trip progress, events, and documentation are consistently recorded.

This system will form the operational foundation for a future **Logistics ERP platform**.
